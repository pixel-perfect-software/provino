# @provino/dialect-vitest

The Vitest 5.0 dialect (B14a: T1.8's Vitest half, T11.1, T11.2). Policy values, the names of the globals Vitest injects with `globals: true`, the hoisting rules and the type entries. The config reader, the CLI flags and the `vitest` facade are T11.15 and T11.16 (Phase 2); until then `provino` doesn't list this dialect among its built-ins, so no run reaches the stubs. Follow `.claude/rules/dialects.md`.

## Vitest's `expect` (B25b, T7.6, D170)

- **The front end is `@provino/expect`'s; the matchers are `@vitest/expect`'s.** `loadVitestPlugins` runs the vendored `JestChaiExpect`, `ChaiStyleAssertions` and `JestAsymmetricMatchers` once, over a stand-in `chai` whose `Assertion.prototype` is `VitestAssertion`'s and whose `expect` collects the asymmetric factories. Each `def`'d method (aliases and sinon-chai's method names included) becomes a matcher that runs it on a fresh `VitestAssertion` holding Chai's flags in `__flags` (`object`, `negate`, `promise`, `message`), so Chai's own `getMessage`, `inspect` and `assert` work on it unchanged. It passes when the method returns; what it throws (a Chai `AssertionError` for a failure) goes to the engine, which anchors it at the user's call and holds it under `expect.soft`; `vitestShapeTestError` gives it Vitest's `diff` where the run reports it. The flags are a plain object literal, and `VitestAssertion` keeps them under a symbol behind a prototype `__flags` accessor (Chai calls its methods on a Proxy of the assertion, which a private field refuses). The stand-in's `getMessage` words a message with no `#{…}` placeholder itself, so a passing matcher that words its failure first (`toHaveBeenCalled`) loads no Chai. `toSatisfy` and `toBeOneOf` are built-ins here with their asymmetric forms, as `expect.extend` gives them in Vitest; a custom matcher's failure is Vitest's `JestExtendError`. The snapshot matchers are the engine's, with `toMatchFileSnapshot`; `snapshot.mismatch: 'throw'` makes a mismatch throw at the call (D61).
- **Chai loads only when needed** (`expect/chai.ts`: a synchronous `require` of Chai 6, an ES module with no top-level await): on a failure (to word it and make Chai's `AssertionError`), on one of the four matchers that are Chai's own assertions upstream (`toContain` on an array or string, `toBeInstanceOf`, `toHaveLength`, `toThrow` without a class: Vitest calls `this.contain`, `instanceOf`, `have.length`, `throws`), and on a Chai chain. Building the table and passing assertions load none (`internal/lazy-chai.ts`).
- **Chai chains** (`expect(x).to.equal(1)`, `expect(spy).called`): any name that is no matcher and not on `Object.prototype` (`then` excepted) reaches the table's `chain`, which builds a real Chai `Assertion` (Chai with Vitest's plugins on its own prototype, set up once) through `.resolves`/`.rejects` and `.not` as the call came and reads the name from it; an unknown name throws Chai's `Invalid Chai property`, as in Vitest. A chain is counted with its `expect()` call (`assertionCalls: 'per-expect-call'`, below) and is not held under `expect.soft` (a limit). A matcher that passes returns the assertion it was read from (the engine does so for a table with a `chain`), so `expect(1).toBe(1).to.equal(1)` goes on, loading Chai only when a chain is read; a custom matcher's `this.equals` is Vitest's (`ExpectTable.equals`), and `this.soft`/`this.poll` are set as Vitest's matcher state sets them. The plugins' `throw`/`throws`/`Throw` overwrite wraps Chai's own methods, taken as Chai loads (`chai.ts`'s `chaiOriginal`), before Vitest's plugin can go on Chai's prototype, so `.rejects.toThrow('msg')` unwraps once after a chain was read.
- **Assertions are counted per `expect()` call** (`expect.assertionCalls: 'per-expect-call'`, Vitest's `createExpect`, `fixtures/expect/policies/assertions-per-expect`): a bare `expect(x)` counts, one `expect(x)` used for two matchers counts once, a matcher that throws still counted, an `expect.poll` once.
- **`expect(actual, message)`** takes a message (`expect.messageArgument: true`, `expect/messages/extend-and-misuse`), which prefixes a failure as Chai's `message` flag does. `.resolves`/`.rejects` check their value as they are read (`expect.settlementCheck: 'at-read'`, `expect/messages/promises`).
- **Errors and stacks** follow Vitest: a failure is `AssertionError: …` at the matcher's call; under `.resolves`/`.rejects` the error carries the stack of the `.resolves` read under an `Error:` header (Vitest's `new Error('resolves')`), the value is checked as `.resolves` is read (`You must provide a Promise …`, a `TypeError`), `.rejects` of a function calls it then, and an unawaited assertion is reported at the read too.
- **Tests:** `runtime/expect-table.test.ts` holds the table to the real `@vitest/expect` (reached from `vitest`, a devDependency, in a child: it defines the matcher slot as it loads) over 84 cases (pass or fail, the error's name, message, diff and cause; a case may be a script of its own: a chain after a matcher, `.rejects.toThrow` after a Chai chain, a wrong settlement, a custom matcher's `this`, a value tagged as another class), the matcher and asymmetric names, and that Chai loads on the first failure only. `@provino/harness`'s expect corpus holds it to every Vitest message golden, frames included; its policy corpus to the async policies' recordings; its equality corpus to the 31 equality and 18 realm cases.

## Where the values come from

Every value matches Vitest 5.0.5, and its evidence is in `@provino/harness`'s `src/policy-truth/claims/` (T1.7): a golden recorded from the real Vitest, a corpus check a flipped value fails, our own key with its test (`isolationDefault`, the hoisting `id`, `valueFormatter`: `dialect.test.ts` pins them), a value nothing reads, or `pending` with its owner. `policy-truth.test.ts` there fails when a value and its evidence disagree. A recording overruled the research once: `core.errors.hasAssertionsWhenFailed` is `skip` (Vitest checks `expect.hasAssertions` only on a test that passed, `policy/core-outcomes`).

`core.retry.functionCondition` is `ignore-warn` (D7): Vitest 5.0.5 never calls a function `retry.condition` and retries every failure (`runtime/retry-condition-function`), and honouring it could turn a green suite red, so this dialect keeps that outcome and core warns once a file, naming the test and then giving the dialect's own `core.retry.functionConditionWarning` (why, and the fix: Provino's text, pinned by `dialect.test.ts`, not a Vitest message); the neutral and Jest value, `call`, asks the function.

`vitest-list` (`sequence.hooks: 'list'`) is a project config, not a dialect: the harness's corpus profiles layer it over `vitestPolicy`.
